Research

I help build the analysis infrastructure that next-generation sky surveys need, and use it to learn physics. My work spans the chain from detector pixels to cosmological and stellar parameters. It falls into seven tracks: Track 0, AI in science, is the focus of my fellowship; Tracks 1–6 cover images, cosmology and stars. Each track has its own page: a short introduction for newcomers, what I have done, my papers with my contribution to each, and what comes next.

  • 0 · AI in Science: Using physics to improve the data that AI learns from, and making sure that everything AI helps produce can be checked.
  • 1 · Image Reconstruction: Reconstructing well-sampled images with a controlled point spread function from a telescope's undersampled exposures, for the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope and beyond. (7 papers)
  • 2 · Image Analysis: The physical meaning of astronomical images: noise, detector effects and systematics, and images as discrete samples of continuous fields. (4 papers)
  • 3 · Cosmological Inference: What surveys like Roman's can tell us about the Universe, and how to extract it. (2 papers)
  • 4 · Cosmological Simulations: Simulations of cosmic structure, and the numerical methods that run them. (3 papers)
  • 5 · Stellar Modeling: Testing stellar evolution models against thousands of observed red giant stars. (2 papers)
  • 6 · Asteroseismology: Stellar oscillations as precise measures of stellar masses and ages, from Kepler to Roman. (6 papers)
  • ✱ · Miscellany: other work. None yet.

The complete list of papers is on Publications.
