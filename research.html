1 · Image Reconstruction images

Reconstructing well-sampled images with a controlled point spread function from a telescope's undersampled exposures, for the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope and beyond.

I refactored IMCOM, which reconstructs images by combining many dithered exposures, and applied it to the OpenUniverse2024 simulations of Roman. I then developed Effortless (EFFicient Optimal image ReconsTruction using LESS memory), a new algorithm for linear image reconstruction. By avoiding IMCOM's most laborious calculations, it is tens of times faster and uses less memory, and it can also reconstruct a single exposure. The code is released on GitHub.
