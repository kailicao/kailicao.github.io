About · Personal

Personal

A little more about me.

My path

I studied French language and literature at Nanjing University. A summer as a visiting student at MIT in 2019 led to my first research paper, on galaxy clusters in simulations. I then spent a year on computer science at Nanjing before my PhD in physics at The Ohio State University, which took me from red giant stars to the Roman Space Telescope. I am now at the University of Michigan.

What I care about

I want to build bridges: the computational kind, between theory and data, and the human kind, between people and science.

How I work

Since my second year of college, I have tracked how I spend my time. I keep notes on almost every part of my research, which I call work logs, and I read the literature with LSAP, my system of checked notes on every paper I read (see AI in Science).

Languages

Chinese is my native language. I work in English, and I studied French.

My name in Chinese is 曹开力, written in pinyin as Cáo Kāilì. As is usual in Chinese, the family name comes first: Cao (曹) is my family name, and Kaili (开力) is my given name, which I put first in English. Cao sounds roughly like "tsow" (rhyming with "now"), and Kaili like "kye-lee".
