4 · Cosmological Simulations cosmology

Simulations of cosmic structure, and the numerical methods that run them.

In brief

Simulations connect theory with observation, but only if they are compared like with like: a simulated galaxy cluster has to be "observed" the way a real one is before the two can be compared. The numerical methods inside simulations matter as well, because they decide what a simulation conserves and how far it can be trusted.

What I have done

My first research project, as a visiting undergraduate student at MIT in 2019, used the Mock-X framework to study how galaxy clusters are classified as dynamically relaxed. We examined 9 commonly used morphological metrics in 54,000 synthetic X-ray images of clusters from the IllustrisTNG, BAHAMAS and MACSIS simulations, and found that relaxed subsets defined by different metrics agree poorly, so selecting "relaxed" clusters introduces significant selection effects (Cao et al. 2021).

I also developed ContEvol, a family of implicit numerical methods that solve only linear equations and are almost symplectic. They give continuous solutions and handle problems from orbits to the Schrödinger equation (Cao 2025; code).
